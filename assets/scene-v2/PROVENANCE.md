# Photographic edition assets — 11 October 2026

Generated using the built-in image_gen tool (default built-in mode; no CLI/API fallback). These are original generated visual assets, not photographs of Stefan’s actual desk or possessions. The accessories are part of the photographic environment; the book is live Three.js geometry.

Prompt directions (summarized):

- `desk-photograph.webp`: photorealistic overhead editorial walnut desk, believable fine grain and warm directional natural light; ivory coffee cup at upper right, mechanical watch at left, pen at lower left; generous empty center for a live book; no text, logo or drawn book.
- `desk-portrait.webp`: matching portrait 9:16 environment, coffee upper right, watch lower left, pen lower right; clear central book area and photographic shadows/material detail.
- `leather-scan.webp`: seamless macro espresso-black calfskin leather surface, fine irregular natural pores, even illumination, no text, object, seams or logos; used as cover color and bump detail.

Generated source PNGs are retained in the delivery’s `source-assets` directory. Production assets are compressed WebP derivatives (landscape width 2560; portrait height 1800; leather 1536 square), processed with Sharp. No third-party branding or stock licensing is asserted. `paper-grain.svg` is a deterministic procedural grain overlay authored in the repository.

Page artwork under `assets/book-pages` is generated by Playwright from the site’s actual bilingual HTML, local licensed fonts and existing project captures. It is not AI-generated project imagery. Its manifest hashes all design/content inputs; CI rejects stale or overflowing artwork.
